# Finance Futures

Build a premium, highly credible financial-careers landing page called JUSTFIN.

JUSTFIN is a modern remote-jobs and financial-careers platform that connects qualified candidates with remote and flexible employment opportunities in finance, banking, fintech, accounting, operations, customer support, compliance, technology, data, and related professional fields.

The website should feel like a combination of a premium financial institution, executive recruitment platform, and modern fintech career marketplace.

IMPORTANT BRAND/LEGAL PRESENTATION

Do NOT represent JUSTFIN as JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citigroup, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, U.S. Bancorp, Capital One, PNC, BNY, Truist, TD Bank, Charles Schwab, State Street, American Express, or Fifth Third Bank.

Do NOT state or imply that any of these companies sponsor, endorse, partner with, employ through, or officially operate JUSTFIN unless explicit authorization is provided.

Instead, create a section titled:

"Financial Industry Opportunities"

and another section titled:

"Companies & Institutions Candidates May Encounter"

Use company names/logos only where legally appropriate and clearly label the section:

"Independent career platform — not affiliated with or endorsed by the institutions displayed."

Where logos cannot legally be used, use elegant text-based company cards instead.
